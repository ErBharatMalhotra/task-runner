What is Bharat DN?

Bharat DN is a network of small, sharp websites that each do one job well, instead of one giant site that does everything badly. The root domain, bharatdn.com, is the live index of that network — it shows what changed today, which hubs are live, and where to go next.

Every hub runs on the same backbone: the same design system, the same identity, and the same automated daily pipeline that refreshes content without manual editing.

The network

  • bharatdn.com — News, daily digest and the network index (this site)
  • ielts.bharatdn.com — IELTS preparation: mock tests, band trackers, study plans
  • jobs.bharatdn.com — Jobs board indexed across the live city network
  • tools.bharatdn.com — Curated utilities for Indian creators and professionals
  • news.bharatdn.com — Long-form editorial and analysis

Our mission

The Indian internet is growing fast, but useful information is scattered across dozens of cluttered pages. Bharat DN exists to fix that: fast, independent, ad-supported sites that respect your time — no login walls, no clickbait, no dark patterns.

How this site is made

This site is honest about its machinery: an automated pipeline regenerates the digest, statistics and activity feed every day, and the results are published statically — no server, no database, no tracking wall.

Editorial process: city guides are assembled with a mix of editorial writing and assisted drafting tools, then reviewed and checked against primary sources before publishing. Source attribution is given wherever material is summarised.

Editorial values

  • Accuracy over speed. A story that is wrong is worse than a story that is late.
  • Sources, always. Claims are linked back to where they came from.
  • Corrections welcome. If something is off, tell us and we will fix it — see Contact.
  • Independence. No advertiser or partner decides what gets published.

Who runs it

Bharat DN is built and maintained independently by Bharat Malhotraa. It is funded by display advertising, which keeps every hub free to use.
