How it works

  1. Message us — WhatsApp the button above (message is pre-filled) or email info@bharatdn.com, with your city, business name, phone/WhatsApp and the placement you want.

Our rules (the short version)

  • Paid placements are always labelled "Sponsored" and use rel="sponsored" — we never sell dofollow links or disguise ads as editorial.
  • Buying a slot never changes what the guide says — ratings, reviews and recommendations stay independent.
  • We can decline any business or category, and slots are capped per city to keep the page useful.
  • Slots run for a month at minimum; unexpired slots are removed automatically at expiry — no surprise renewals.
  • No guaranteed number of calls or enquiries — we show you the real numbers, not promises.
