About the Punjab network

The combined population of these 4 cities stood at roughly 39,10,800 in Census 2011. Every city page follows the same verified format: census-grade facts, four-hour live weather and air-quality, and on-ground specifics — stations, pincode heads, local food streets, weekend getaways — written for that exact town, never templated fluff.

Bharat DN is a network: from any Punjab city page you can reach that city's jobs board, national news digest and free tools without losing your place. City pages for the rest of Punjab — including smaller district towns — are authored continuously and appear here the day they clear verification.
