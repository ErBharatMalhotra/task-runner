Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Bharat Digital Network collects, how it is used, and what choices you have — in plain language.

Effective: 26 September 2026

1. Who we are

Bharat Digital Network ("Bharat DN", "we", "us") operates the website bharatdn.com and its associated hubs. Questions about this policy can be sent to info@bharatdn.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us — if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in the message. We use it only to reply.
  • Log data — like most websites, our servers and hosting provider automatically record information such as IP address, browser type, referring pages and the time of your visit. This is used for security, abuse prevention and aggregate statistics.
  • Analytics — we may use privacy-focused, aggregated analytics to understand which pages are useful. We do not build individual profiles.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Bharat DN itself uses only essential cookies (for example, to remember basic preferences). Third-party services — in particular our advertising partner — may set their own cookies as described below. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time; the site remains usable without them.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

Advertising disclosure: this site displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Ads keep every Bharat DN hub free to use.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies via www.aboutads.info or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA) tool.

Google's use of cookies and how partners use cookies is covered in Google's documents: How Google uses cookies and How Google uses data when you use partners' sites or apps.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (link above), or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices.

5. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the website
  • To respond to messages you send us
  • To detect spam, fraud and abuse
  • To produce aggregated statistics (never to identify you personally)

6. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Data may be shared only with: (a) service providers who help run the site (hosting, email, analytics, advertising) under their own privacy terms, or (b) authorities when required by law.

7. Data retention and security

We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle it and maintain basic records. We apply reasonable technical measures (HTTPS, access controls, static publishing with no user database) — but no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location (including GDPR in the EEA/UK and CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any right, email info@bharatdn.com — we respond within 30 days.

9. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Links to other sites

Bharat DN links to other sites (including our own hubs and, occasionally, external sources). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies — review them on those sites.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective" date at the top will always show the latest revision. Material changes will be announced in the daily digest on the homepage.

12. Contact

For any privacy question: info@bharatdn.com. See also our Terms of Use and Disclaimer.
