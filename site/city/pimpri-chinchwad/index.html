Pimpri-Chinchwad in one minute

Pimpri-Chinchwad — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Pimpri-Chinchwad is a major industrial city governed by one of Asia—s wealthiest municipal corporations. Located northwest of Pune, it emerged from a cluster of small villages into a massive urban sprawl following the establishment of the Maharashtra Industrial Development Corporation in the 1960s. The city serves as a critical manufacturing node, hosting global giants in the automotive, engineering, and information technology sectors while maintaining distinct cultural pockets.

The Pavana River bisects the twin townships, providing a geographic anchor for residential and industrial zones. While historically an industrial satellite, the city has developed its own modern infrastructure, including the Pune Metro and extensive flyover networks. It is home to the historic Morya Gosavi Temple, marking it as a site of spiritual significance for the Ganapatya sect alongside its reputation for heavy machinery and technical innovation.

FAQ

Is Pimpri-Chinchwad different from Pune?

It is a separate municipal city with its own administration, though it is part of the Pune Metropolitan Region and shares a continuous urban landscape.

What is the city best known for?

The city is globally recognized as a major hub for the automotive industry and for the historic Morya Gosavi Ganpati temple.

How is the connectivity to Mumbai?

It is excellently connected via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway and the Old Highway, with several intercity trains stopping at Chinchwad and Pimpri stations.

What are the major residential areas?

Developed residential pockets include Pradhikaran, Wakad, Sangvi, and Pimple Saudagar, which are popular due to their proximity to IT hubs and industrial zones.
