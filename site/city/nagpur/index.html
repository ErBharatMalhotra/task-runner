Nagpur in one minute

Nagpur — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Nagpur sits at India’s surveyed centre — the British Zero Mile benchmark still stands in the old town, and the country’s north–south and east–west corridors cross under it. Second capital of Maharashtra, where the winter Assembly session runs, it is Vidarbha’s metropolis: a rail junction that India’s freight plan treats as a hinge, and orchards of Nagpur santra that reach every winter table.

Work and study pull the same crowd — mining and coal country behind it, defence works and MIHAN’s airport SEZ on its edge, VNIT and a cluster of medical and law campuses inside it. Within a drive: Deekshabhoomi’s great stupa, Sitabuldi’s fort ridge, the lakes at Futala and Ambazari — and Pench, Tadoba and Ramtek where the Vidarbha forest begins. Nagpur (NGP) on the Howrah–Mumbai trunk, Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar airport (NAG) ~9 km, metro running on the north–south line.

FAQ

What is Nagpur famous for?

The Orange City — Nagpur santra citrus, the Zero Mile centre-of-India marker, Deekshabhoomi’s stupa and Maharashtra’s winter Assembly.

What is the population of Nagpur?

As per Census 2011, Nagpur city has 24,97,870 residents (municipal corporation); the district has about 46 lakh.

Which are the best places to visit there?

Deekshabhoomi, Zero Mile, Sitabuldi Fort, Futala and Ambazari lakes — with Ramtek, Pench and Tadoba within a day’s drive.

How do I reach Nagpur?

Nagpur (NGP) is a major railway junction on the Howrah–Mumbai trunk, Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar airport (NAG) ~9 km out, and metro on the north–south line.
