Bathinda in one minute

Bathinda — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Bathinda guards one of India’s oldest surviving forts — the Qila Mubarak, its 10th-century brick towers holding the cell where Empress Razia Sultan was imprisoned in 1240. The Malwa city grew around the rail yards of Bathinda Junction — once counted among Asia’s biggest — and the grain mandis of the cotton-wheat belt. Census 2011 counted about 2.9 lakh in the corporation.

The modern city works in power and grain — the Guru Nanak Dev thermal plant, the HPCL-Mittal refinery orbit and the district’s cotton-ginning floors — while the Central University of Punjab’s campus, the Rose Garden weeks and the Lakhi Jungle shrine fill the leisure map.

FAQ

What is Bathinda famous for?

The Qila Mubarak — one of India’s oldest surviving forts with Razia Sultan’s imprisonment legend — the giant BTI rail junction and the thermal-refinery belt.

What is the population of Bathinda?

Census 2011: about 2,85,813 in the corporation city (queue figure 2,85,813); UA ~3,26,000; Bathinda district 13,88,525.

What is the pincode of Bathinda?

The city starts at 151001; Bathinda spans 151001–151402 across Model Town, the cantonment and the old city.

The Qila Mubarak towers, the Rose Garden weeks and the Lakhi Jungle shrine — with Talwandi Sabo’s Damdama Sahib as the takht run.
