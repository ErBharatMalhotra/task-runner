Pune in one minute

Pune — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Pune, seat of the Peshwas, was the Maratha capital when Shaniwar Wada ruled the Deccan from 1732 — a city of forts’ shadow and reform: Savitribai Phule opened India’s first girls’ school here in 1848. Census 2011 counted 31 lakh in the city, 50.5 lakh in the UA — with merger-era estimates past 70 lakh. The Oxford of the East keeps Fergusson, SPPU and COEP full of the country’s students.

The new Pune codes — Hinjawadi’s Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park and the Kharadi EON belt — while weekends climb to Sinhagad, Lonavala or the Aga Khan Palace’s Gandhi memorials. Wada-culture food (misal, puran poli), the Deccan Gymkhana clubs and the Ganesh-pandals of the old city hold the two Punes together.

FAQ

What is Pune famous for?

The Peshwa capital’s Shaniwar Wada, Savitribai Phule’s first girls’ school, the Oxford-of-the-East campus belt, Hinjawadi’s IT park and the grand Ganesh Utsav.

What is the population of Pune?

Census 2011: 31,24,458 in the PMC city and 50,57,709 in the UA; post-2011 merger estimates run past 70 lakh (pending).

What is the pincode of Pune?

The old city starts at 411001; the metro spans 411001–411062 plus the Pimpri-Chinchwad 4110xx belt.

Which places should I visit there?

Shaniwar Wada and Aga Khan Palace, Parvati Hill and the Pataleshwar caves — with a Sinhagad sunrise or Lonavala weekend on top.
