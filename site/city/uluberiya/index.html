Uluberiya in one minute

Uluberiya — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Uluberiya is a significant industrial city and administrative headquarters of the Uluberiya subdivision in the Howrah district. Situated on the western bank of the Hooghly River, it serves as a critical junction on the South Eastern Railway. The city is characterized by its historical role as a river port and its contemporary identity as a manufacturing base for logistics, garments, and heavy industries within the Kolkata Metropolitan Area.

The Hooghly River defines the city geography, providing both a scenic waterfront and a historical trade route. Uluberiya is renowned for its football culture and its proximity to the industrial clusters of Fuleswar and Chengail. The local economy is driven by a mix of traditional small-scale manufacturing, such as shuttlecock making and embroidery, alongside modern industrial parks that leverage the city connectivity to the National Highway 16.

FAQ

Is Uluberiya part of Kolkata?

Uluberiya is part of the Kolkata Metropolitan Area (KMA) but functions as a separate municipality and administrative headquarters within the Howrah district.

What is the best way to reach Uluberiya?

The city is best reached by local trains from Howrah Station, taking approximately one hour, or by road via National Highway 16.

What are the major industries here?

The economy is dominated by the WBIIDC Industrial Growth Centre, logistics, garment manufacturing, and the famous local shuttlecock making industry.

What is famous at Garchumuk?

Garchumuk is a popular picnic spot nearby, known for the Atanna Gate (58-gate) barrage on the Damodar River and its scenic riverine views.
