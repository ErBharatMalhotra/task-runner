Vellore in one minute

Vellore — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

The Vellore Fort stands as the city central landmark, a 16th-century granite structure surrounded by a deep moat that has hosted the Vijayanagara, Bijapur, Maratha, and British administrations. It is historically significant as the site of the 1806 Vellore Mutiny, the first major uprising against British rule in India. Today, the city serves as a critical administrative headquarters for the northern region of Tamil Nadu.

Modern Vellore is internationally recognized for the Christian Medical College (CMC), which attracts patients from across South Asia. The city economy is a powerhouse of leather manufacturing and export, contributing significantly to India total leather output. Nearby, the sprawling Sripuram Golden Temple, coated in fifteen hundred kilograms of pure gold, has transformed the local landscape into a major spiritual destination.

Hidden & heritage places in Vellore

Vellore beyond the postcards — quiet places and lanes most guides skip.

City center · Moat

Vellore Fort Museum

Housed within the historic fort, this museum displays stone carvings, ancient weaponry, and artifacts from the Pallava and Chola dynasties. The Jalakandeswarar Temple inside the fort features intricate Vijayanagara era stone masonry.

8 km · Southern hills

Sripuram Golden Temple

A spiritual park situated at the foot of small hills, featuring a star-shaped path leading to a temple covered in gold leaf. It is dedicated to the goddess Narayani and maintains vast landscaped gardens.

12 km · Hilltop

Ratnagiri Murugan Temple

A 14th-century temple dedicated to Lord Murugan, extensively renovated in the 20th century. It sits atop a hillock providing panoramic views of the Palar river valley and the National Highway 48.

Directions ↗
City center · Ida Scudder Rd

CMC Campus

The historic campus of the Christian Medical College represents the city medical heritage. The architecture reflects early 20th-century colonial styles and serves as a landmark for patients traveling from across the globe.

Directions ↗
25 km · Forested valley

Amirthi Zoological Park

Split between a tourist zone and a protected forest, this park features local wildlife such as spotted deer and crocodiles. It is a popular spot for trekking during the monsoon season.

30 km · Hill base

Virinjipuram Margabandeeswarar

An ancient temple known for its elaborate Dravidian architecture and a unique stone lion sculpture that serves as a sundial. It is associated with the transit of the planet Saturn.

Festival & fair calendar

January

Pongal

The harvest festival is celebrated with great fervor, featuring traditional cooking of new rice and cattle worship in the surrounding villages.

July

Aadi Perukku

A monsoon festival celebrated along the banks of the Palar river and local tanks to honor the life-giving properties of water.

April

Tamil New Year

Marked by special prayers at the Jalakandeswarar Temple and the preparation of Maanga Pachadi to signify the different flavors of life.

November

Karthigai Deepam

The festival of lights involves lighting earthen lamps across homes and atop the nearby hills, reflecting the region spiritual traditions.

FAQ

Which is the main railway station in Vellore?

The primary station is Katpadi Junction (KPD), located about 7 km from the city center. It is a major stop on the Chennai-Bangalore and Chennai-Coimbatore rail routes.

Why is Vellore famous for healthcare?

Vellore is home to the Christian Medical College (CMC), a premier medical institution founded by Ida S. Scudder. It is world-renowned for its high-quality treatment and medical research.

Is Vellore safe for tourists?

Yes, Vellore is a safe and conservative city. It is well-connected by road and rail, with a high presence of students and medical professionals creating a cosmopolitan atmosphere.

What is the best time to visit Vellore?

The best time to visit is from October to February during the winter season when the weather is pleasant for sightseeing at the Fort and Golden Temple.
