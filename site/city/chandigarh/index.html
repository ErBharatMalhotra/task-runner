Chandigarh in one minute

India’s first planned city — a sector grid at the Shivalik foothills, joint capital of two states.

Chandigarh, India’s first planned city after Independence, sits at the foot of the Shivalik hills as the joint capital of Punjab and Haryana. Le Corbusier laid it out on a grid of 47 sectors — each with its own market, park and green belt, stitched together by wide axial roads. Two set-pieces define the skyline: Nek Chand’s Rock Garden, built in secret from factory waste from 1957 and opened in 1976, and Sukhna Lake, a 1958 reservoir against the hills where bar-headed geese land every winter.

The city runs the governments of two states — twin secretariats, the Punjab and Haryana High Court and Raj Bhavan sit inside one sector plan — alongside Panjab University (1882), PGIMER and a hospital belt that draws patients from across the region. The Capitol Complex, Le Corbusier’s concrete capitol with the Open Hand monument, was inscribed by UNESCO in 2016. Manimajra’s IT Park carries the software and BPO floors, while Chandigarh airport (IXC), Chandigarh Junction (CDG) and NH-44 keep the tricity moving — Delhi is ~245 km to the south.

Festival & fair calendar

Rose Festival

Three days at Zakir Hussain Rose Garden — thousands of roses, stalls, live stages and the city’s biggest garden crowd.

Bonfires in sector parks on 13 January — Punjab’s winter night of rewri, peanuts and bhangra.

FAQ

Why is Chandigarh famous?

For being India’s first planned city after Independence — Le Corbusier’s sector grid, the UNESCO-listed Capitol Complex, Nek Chand’s Rock Garden and Sukhna Lake at the Shivalik edge.

What is the population of Chandigarh?

As per Census 2011, Chandigarh city has 10,26,545 residents; the urban agglomeration is 16,20,167 and the union-territory district 10,55,450.

What are the places to visit there?

Rock Garden, Sukhna Lake, Capitol Complex, Zakir Hussain Rose Garden and Sector 17; easy trips to Pinjore (~22 km), Morni Hills (~35 km) and Shimla (~120 km).

How do I reach Chandigarh?

Chandigarh Junction (CDG), Chandigarh International Airport (IXC) ~9 km away, and NH-44 road links — Delhi is ~245 km to the south.
