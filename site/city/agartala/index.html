Agartala in one minute

Agartala — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Agartala, the Manikya dynasty’s planned capital, keeps its royal grace — the 1901 Ujjayanta Palace (now the state museum) with its Mughal-garden domes, the Jagannath temple’s octagonal spires and the Kunjaban palace lawns. Census 2011 counted about 4.0 lakh in the corporation, 4.7 lakh in the UA, on the plains a bowshot from the Bangladesh border.

The city works in bamboo, rubber and loom — Tripura’s bamboo-craft economy, the incense-stick units and the state’s big rubber belt feed its bazaars, while the new Agartala-Akhaura rail link and the Maharaja Bir Bikram airport stitch it into the Bengal-Bangladesh grid. Neermahal’s lake palace and Unakoti’s rock gods orbit the map.

FAQ

What is Agartala famous for?

The Ujjayanta Palace museum, the Jagannath temple, Tripura’s bamboo-and-rubber economy and the Neermahal lake palace a short drive away.

What is the population of Agartala?

Census 2011: about 3,99,688 in the corporation city (queue figure 4,04,157, pending reconciliation); UA 4,70,480; West Tripura 17,24,619 pre-split.

What is the pincode of Agartala?

The city starts at 799001; Agartala spans 799001–799300 across Battala, Krishnanagar and the airport belt.

Which places should I visit there?

Ujjayanta’s galleries, the Jagannath shrine and the craft bazaars — with Neermahal, Sepahijala and Unakoti as the day runs.
