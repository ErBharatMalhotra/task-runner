Aizawl in one minute

Aizawl — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

The capital of Mizoram is a high-altitude city situated on a north-south ridge overlooking the Tlawng River valley. Established as a colonial military outpost in 1890, it has evolved into the primary hub for Mizo culture, administration, and education. The city is characterized by its vertical architecture and steep winding roads that define its urban landscape.

As a center for Christian heritage and traditional Mizo customs, Aizawl hosts numerous theological colleges and the Mizoram State Museum. The economy is driven by government services, bamboo handicrafts, and mountain agriculture. The city serves as the vital transit point for the mountainous interior of Northeast India.

FAQ

What is the best time to visit Aizawl?

The ideal months are from October to March when the weather is cool and clear, avoiding the heavy monsoon rains that can disrupt hill travel.

How do I reach Aizawl by air?

Lengpui Airport is the primary gateway, located about 32 kilometers from the city. It has direct flight connections to Kolkata, Guwahati, and Imphal.

What are the local transport options?

Taxis are the primary mode of transport within the city. There are no auto-rickshaws due to the steep terrain, and city buses ply on specific main routes.

Is an Inner Line Permit required?

Yes, domestic Indian tourists require an Inner Line Permit (ILP) to enter Mizoram, which can be obtained at airport counters or state liaison offices.
