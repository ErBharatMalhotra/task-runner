Prayagraj in one minute

The Sangam city — Ganga, Yamuna and the hidden Saraswati, with Kumbh, courts and the doab’s university town.

Prayagraj, called Allahabad until 2018, stands where the Ganga and Yamuna meet — the Sangam, with the Saraswati believed to join them under the silt. Akbar raised the fort here in 1583, and the old city behind its walls still runs on the bazaar lanes of Katra, Chowk and Daraganj, boats at first light and the ghats that fill for the Magh Mela every January.

It is a judicial and administrative capital (Allahabad High Court, sitting since 1866, and the state public service commission), an education heavyweight (University of Allahabad, 1887, long called the Oxford of the East) and the home of Anand Bhavan, the Nehru family house. The Kumbh cycle at the Sangam, the junction on the Delhi–Howrah main line, Bamrauli airport (IXD) and the NH-19 corridor keep the rest moving — Kanpur is ~200 km west, Varanasi ~125 km east.

Festival & fair calendar

Jan–Feb

Magh Mela

The annual month-long congregation at the Sangam — camps, akhadas and the full-moon bath.

12-year cycle

Kumbh Mela

The Sangam’s great gathering — the 2025 Mahakumbh was the last here, and the belt starts preparing again.

Oct–Nov

Chhath Puja

Arail and Sangam banks fill at dawn and dusk for the sun offering — the city’s biggest river festival.

Feb–Mar

Holi

Gulal, drums and processions through the old lanes; Barsana’s lathmar is a couple of hours away.

Mar–Apr

Ram Navami & Hanuman Jayanti

Night-long queues at Lete Hanuman by the fort ghats and processions from the old city.

Best season

Oct – Mar

Winter on the Sangam — mela months, clear river light and festival season together.

FAQ

Why is Prayagraj famous?

For the Sangam of the Ganga and Yamuna, the Kumbh and Magh Mela, the Allahabad High Court and the University of Allahabad — plus Anand Bhavan, the Nehru family home.

What is the population of Prayagraj?

As per Census 2011, Prayagraj city has 11,12,511 residents; the urban agglomeration is 12,16,719 and the district 36,37,032.

What are the places to visit there?

The Sangam and boat ghats, Allahabad Fort with Akshayavat, Khusro Bagh, Chandrashekhar Azad Park, Anand Bhavan and All Saints Cathedral.

How do I reach Prayagraj?

Prayagraj Junction (PRYJ) on the Delhi–Howrah main line, Bamrauli airport (IXD) ~15 km away, and the NH-19 road corridor.
