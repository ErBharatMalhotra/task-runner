Noida in one minute

Noida — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Noida — the New Okhla Industrial Development Authority’s 1976-planned city — grew from authority plots into one of NCR’s working cores: IT and BPO campuses across Sectors 62–63, the Film City studios that put India’s news channels here, and the mall-and-office grid along the DND Flyway. Census 2011 counted about 6.4 lakh; the Noida-Greater Noida UA now crosses ten lakh.

The city keeps green gaps in the concrete — the Okhla Bird Sanctuary on the Yamuna edge, the Botanical Garden station’s parklands and the sector parks — while the expressway orbit (Greater Noida, the Yamuna Expressway, the Jewar airport belt) keeps stretching its geography. The Aqua Line metro and the supertech-demolition headlines write its modern chapters.

FAQ

What is Noida famous for?

The 1976-planned NCR city — India’s TV-news and media hub, IT/BPO campuses, the DND Flyway and the Okhla Bird Sanctuary on the Yamuna edge.

What is the population of Noida?

Census 2011: about 6,42,381 (queue figure 6,37,078, pending reconciliation); the Noida-Greater Noida UA now crosses ten lakh; district 16,74,714.

What is the pincode of Noida?

The city starts at 201301; Noida spans 201301–201309 across Sector belts and the Phase-2 industrial area.

Which places should I visit there?

The Sector-18 mall circuit, Okhla sanctuary’s birding hides, Brahmaputra market’s food rows — with the Buddh circuit as the day run.
