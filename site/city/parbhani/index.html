Parbhani in one minute

Parbhani — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Parbhani, historically known as Prabhavatinagar, is a major agricultural and educational hub in the Marathwada region of Maharashtra. Situated on the banks of the Godavari river basin, it serves as the administrative headquarters for the district. The city is globally recognized for the Vasantrao Naik Marathwada Krishi Vidyapeeth, one of India—s premier agricultural universities, which has pioneered research in sorghum and cotton cultivation across the Deccan plateau.

The cultural landscape of the city is defined by a harmonious blend of traditions, most notably centered around the Hazrat Turabul Haq Dargah. As a critical junction on the South Central Railway, it connects the agricultural hinterlands to major urban centers like Mumbai and Hyderabad. The local economy is driven primarily by soybean processing, cotton ginning, and large-scale grain trade, reflecting its status as a vital market yard for the surrounding fertile plains.

FAQ

What is Parbhani famous for?

It is primarily famous for the Vasantrao Naik Marathwada Krishi Vidyapeeth and the Hazrat Turabul Haq Dargah, making it a center for both science and spirituality.

Is there an airport in Parbhani?

Parbhani does not have its own functional commercial airport; the nearest air connectivity is available at Shri Guru Gobind Singh Ji Airport in Nanded.

Which river flows through Parbhani?

The Dudhna River flows near the city, while the major Godavari River passes through the southern part of the Parbhani district.

How to reach Parbhani?

The city is best reached by train via the Parbhani Junction, which is well-connected to Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Nagpur through the South Central Railway.
