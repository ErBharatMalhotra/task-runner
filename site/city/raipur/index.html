Raipur in one minute

Raipur — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Raipur is the capital and commercial engine of Chhattisgarh — a state only as old as 2000, with a city that has traded rice and timber for two centuries. Set on the Mahanadi plain beside the Kharun river, it sits at the meeting point of the Mumbai–Howrah rail trunk and the region’s steel belt, and its mandis price the paddy harvest of central India.

The economy is heavy and grainy at once — steel re-rolling and power around the city, the Bhilai Steel Plant on its western flank, cement and coal through the surrounding districts, and NIT, AIIMS, IIM and IIIT campuses inside it. A planned extension, Atal Nagar (Naya Raipur), carries the government and IT quarter 17 km south. Within reach: the Buddhist stacks of Sirpur, the Rajiv Lochan temple at Rajim and the forest safari at Nandan Van.

FAQ

What is Raipur famous for?

Chhattisgarh’s capital and Central India’s steel-and-rice trading hub — with Nandan Van safari, Budha Talab and the greenfield Atal Nagar beside it.

What is the population of Raipur?

As per Census 2011, Raipur city has 10,10,433 residents (municipal corporation); the undivided Raipur district counted about 40.6 lakh.

What is the pincode of Raipur?

The main head-post-office pincode is 492001; the wider city uses 492001–492015 series.

How do I reach Raipur?

Raipur Junction (R) on the Mumbai–Howrah trunk, Swami Vivekananda Airport (RPR) ~15 km out, and NH-53/NH-30 by road.
