Balasore in one minute

Balasore — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Balasore, also known as Baleswar, is a key administrative and commercial city in northern Odisha. Situated along the banks of the Budhabalanga River, it has evolved from a historic 17th-century maritime trading outpost of the British, French, and Dutch East India companies into a modern industrial hub. The city serves as the gateway to northern Odisha, bridging the cultural spaces of coastal Bengal and the Odia heartland.

The city is internationally recognized for the Integrated Test Range at Chandipur, where India tests its premier defense missiles. Beyond its strategic military importance, Balasore is a cultural treasure house filled with ancient structures like the Emami Jagannath Temple and the nearby Khirachora Gopinatha shrine, drawing thousands of spiritual travelers and beach lovers annually to its unique receding coastline.

FAQ

What makes Chandipur Beach in Balasore unique?

Chandipur Beach is globally famous because its waters recede up to five kilometers during daily low tides, allowing visitors to walk on the wet ocean floor.

How is Balasore connected by rail?

Balasore Railway Station is a major stop on the Chennai-Howrah main line, offering direct superfast train connectivity to Kolkata, Bhubaneswar, and Chennai.

What is the local culinary specialty of the region?

The most famous local specialty is the Khira of Remuna, a delicious condensed milk sweet offered at the Gopinatha temple.

Can civilians visit the Chandipur missile test range?

No, the core Integrated Test Range is a highly secure DRDO defense facility closed to the public, though civilians can access the nearby public beach.
