Hyderabad in one minute

Hyderabad — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Hyderabad, the City of Pearls, was founded in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah around the Charminar — four minarets over the old bazaar where the world’s diamonds once traded through Golconda (the Kohinoor passed through). The Nizams made it India’s richest princely state; their Chowmahalla, Falaknuma and Osmania institutions still set the city’s grandeur. Census 2011 counted 68 lakh in the GHMC city, over 77 lakh in the UA.

The 1990s flipped it — HITEC City and Genome Valley made it a global tech-and-pharma address, Ramoji Film City the world’s largest studio complex. Between the two worlds: Hussain Sagar’s Buddha statue, Irani chai, and the biryani houses of the old city — Deccani culture in one plate.

FAQ

What is Hyderabad famous for?

The Charminar and Golconda’s diamond past, Nizam palaces, Hyderabadi biryani and haleem, and the HITEC City tech corridor — the City of Pearls turned tech capital.

What is the population of Hyderabad?

Census 2011: 68,09,970 in the GHMC city and 77,49,334 in the urban agglomeration; post-2011 metro estimates cross 1 crore (est.).

What is the pincode of Hyderabad?

The old city starts at 500001 (Afzal Gunj); the metro spans 500001–500101 plus Secunderabad’s 500003–500100 belt.

Which places should I visit there?

Charminar and Golconda, Chowmahalla and Salar Jung, Hussain Sagar and Birla Mandir — with Ramoji Film City a full-day run.
