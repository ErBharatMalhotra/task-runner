Silvassa in one minute

Silvassa — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

The capital of the Dadra and Nagar Haveli district, Silvassa transitioned from a sleepy cluster of tribal villages into a bustling industrial hub. Under Portuguese rule until its liberation in 1954, the town still retains traces of its Iberian past, visible in the whitewashed walls of the Church of Our Lady of Piety. The city is bordered by Gujarat and Maharashtra, making it a unique cultural melting pot.

Modern Silvassa is defined by its scenic Daman Ganga River banks, sprawling gardens, and a highly lucrative tax-exempt manufacturing sector. It serves as the primary gateway to the rich tribal culture of the Warli people, whose distinctive paintings and folk dances are preserved in local museums and celebrated in annual cultural festivals across the union territory.

FAQ

What is the best way to travel to Silvassa?

The nearest railway station is Vapi in Gujarat, about 17 km away, which is well-connected to Mumbai and Ahmedabad. From Vapi, frequent auto-rickshaws and cabs run to Silvassa.

Is alcohol permitted in Silvassa?

Yes, unlike neighbouring Gujarat which is a dry state, alcohol is legal and sold at concessionary tax rates in the Union Territory of Dadra and Nagar Haveli.

What are the famous souvenirs to buy here?

Authentic Warli paintings made with rice paste on mud-primed cloth, bamboo crafts, tribal wind instruments, and local handloom fabrics are the most popular purchases.

When is the best time to visit?

The winter months from November to March are ideal for sightseeing, as the weather is pleasant and outdoor activities along the Daman Ganga River are highly enjoyable.
