Perumbavoor in one minute

Perumbavoor — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Perumbavoor is a bustling industrial municipality in the Ernakulam district of Kerala, strategically positioned near the foothills of the Western Ghats. Located on the Main Central Road (MC Road), the town serves as the primary gateway to the high ranges of Idukki and the tourist circuits of Munnar. It has evolved from an agrarian settlement into one of the most significant timber and plywood hubs in the country, attracting a large migrant workforce.

The town is bordered by the scenic Periyar River to its north, which historically aided the transport of timber logs from the forest reserves of Malayattoor. Today, Perumbavoor is recognized for its unique multi-cultural social fabric, driven by its massive wood-based industries, extensive agricultural plantations, and its proximity to major historical, ecological, and religious landmarks of central Kerala.

FAQ

What is Perumbavoor famous for?

Perumbavoor is nationally famous as the plywood capital of Kerala, housing hundreds of plywood factories, veneer units, and timber sawmills.

How far is Perumbavoor from Kochi?

Perumbavoor is about 36 kilometers from Kochi city center and about 13 kilometers from Cochin International Airport at Nedumbassery.

Which is the nearest railway station?

The nearest railway stations are Aluva and Angamaly, both located about 16 kilometers away from Perumbavoor town.

What are the main agricultural crops here?

The region is a major producer of natural rubber, nutmeg, black pepper, pineapple, and tapioca, supported by the fertile Periyar river basin.
