Kushinagar in one minute

Kushinagar — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Kushinagar is a significant historical town situated in the eastern part of Uttar Pradesh near the Gandak River. It is globally revered as the place where Gautama Buddha attained Mahaparinirvana. The town serves as a vital node on the Buddhist Circuit, characterized by its serene landscape of excavated ruins, archaeological mounds, and modern temples constructed by various Buddhist nations including Japan, Thailand, and Sri Lanka.

The Archaeological Survey of India maintains the core heritage zone, which includes the iconic Parinirvana Stupa and the Muktabandhana Stupa. Beyond its religious identity, Kushinagar is the administrative headquarters of the Kushinagar District. The recent inauguration of an international airport has enhanced its connectivity, transforming this quiet monastic town into a major gateway for international travelers visiting the Terai region.

FAQ

How do I reach Kushinagar?

Kushinagar is accessible via its International Airport or by road from Gorakhpur, which is 52 km away and has a major railway station connecting to Delhi and Lucknow.

What is the significance of the reclining statue?

The Parinirvana Temple houses a 6.1-meter-long statue of Buddha reclining on his right side, representing his final physical state before liberation from the cycle of rebirth.

Is it safe for international travelers?

Yes, as a major international pilgrimage site, the town has a dedicated tourist police force and infrastructure tailored for global visitors from East and Southeast Asia.

What is the best time to visit?

The ideal time is from October to March when the weather is cool. Many pilgrims also visit during Buddha Purnima in May despite the summer heat.
