Nalgonda in one minute

Nalgonda — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Nalgonda serves as a significant administrative and commercial hub within the Telangana state. Situated on the banks of the Nakau River, the city acts as a gateway to the large-scale irrigation projects of the region. Historically, the area has been part of various dynasties, leaving a legacy of cultural influence in the surrounding Nalgonda district.

The urban landscape is characterized by a mix of traditional residential quarters and expanding commercial zones. The local economy is heavily driven by trade and agriculture, supported by the availability of water from the nearby Nagarjuna Sagar project. This connectivity makes it a vital link for transport between Hyderabad and the southern districts of the state.

FAQ

How to reach Nalgonda?

By train via Nalgonda station or by bus from Hyderabad through the state highway network.

What is the main economy?

The economy is driven by agriculture, handloom weaving, and granite mining in the region.

What are the nearby attractions?

Nagarjuna Sagar Dam and Kolanupaka Temple are the most prominent sites nearby.

Is the city well connected?

Yes, it is well connected by rail and road to Hyderabad and other Telangana towns.
