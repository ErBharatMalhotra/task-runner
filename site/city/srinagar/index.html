Srinagar in one minute

Srinagar — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Srinagar, summer capital of Jammu & Kashmir, spreads across the Jhelum and the famous Dal Lake at 1,585 m — houseboats and shikaras, floating vegetable gardens and the morning market that rows to your boat. Census 2011 counted about 11.8 lakh in the SMC city, 15 lakh in the UA.

The Mughal gardens terrace the hills — Nishat (1633), Shalimar (1619), Chashme Shahi — while Hazratbal’s white shrine and the 600-year cedar columns of Jamia Masjid hold the old city. Pashmina spinning, papier-mâché painting and the Pampore saffron fields keep the crafts alive through winter.

FAQ

What is Srinagar famous for?

Dal Lake’s houseboats and shikaras, the Mughal gardens of Nishat and Shalimar, Hazratbal and Jamia Masjid, pashmina shawls and the saffron fields of nearby Pampore.

What is the population of Srinagar?

Census 2011: about 11,80,570 in the SMC city (queue figure 9,79,170 is the older municipal bound, pending reconciliation) and 12,73,312 in the UA.

What is the pincode of Srinagar?

The city starts at 190001 (Lal Chowk); Srinagar spans 190001–190025 plus the university and airport belts.

How many days are enough for Srinagar?

Three to four — Dal and the gardens one day, the old-city shrines another, plus Gulmarg or Pahalgam as the valley day out.
