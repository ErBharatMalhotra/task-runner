Coimbatore in one minute

Coimbatore — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Coimbatore — Kongu Nadu’s capital — is the Manchester of South India: cotton mills since the 1930s and the world’s densest pump-and-motor cluster (CRI, Sharp, Texmo-legacy) in the foundries of Avinashi Road. Census 2011 counted 10.5 lakh in the corporation, 21.4 lakh in the UA — the merger-era city region runs far larger.

The Nilgiris shadow the city — Marudamalai’s hill temple, the Vellingiri peak, and the Siruvani-fed taps (the famous sweet water). PSG and TNAU run the engineering-and-agri brains; Gandhipuram and Ukkadam run the trade floors; Kovai’s medical hub draws all of Kerala’s western belt.

FAQ

What is Coimbatore famous for?

The Manchester of South India — textile mills, the world’s densest pump-and-motor cluster, PSG and TNAU campuses, Kovai’s medical hub and the Nilgiris at its doorstep.

What is the population of Coimbatore?

Census 2011: 10,50,721 in the corporation and 21,36,916 in the UA (the merged city region is far larger — pending). The district counted 34,58,045.

What is the pincode of Coimbatore?

The city starts at 641001 (Townhall); Coimbatore spans 641001–641114 plus the SIDCO and TNAU belts.

Which places should I visit there?

Marudamalai and the Adiyogi at Isha, TNAU’s botanical garden, Singanallur lake — with Ooty and Valparai as the ghat-hill runs.
