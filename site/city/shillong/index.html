Shillong in one minute

Shillong — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Shillong, the Scotland of the East, spreads across pine ridges at 1,496 m — the Khasi capital of Meghalaya, a British hill station since 1864, still framed by the golf links’ rolling meadows and the Shillong Peak’s 1,965-m lookout. Census 2011 counted about 3.5 lakh in the UA.

The city hums with choirs and cafés — the Shillong Chamber Choir’s homeland, India’s indie-rock stages, the Don Bosco Museum’s seven floors of Northeast cultures — while Ward’s Lake’s rowing boats, the Elephant Falls’ terraces and the Umiam lake’s blue width keep the weekend map full. Bara Bazar’s Khasi stalls and Police Bazar’s cafes run the daily rhythm.

FAQ

What is Shillong famous for?

The Scotland-of-the-East ridges at 1,496 m, the Shillong Chamber Choir, India’s indie-rock venues, Ward’s Lake and the Don Bosco Museum.

What is the population of Shillong?

Census 2011: UA about 3,54,759 with the municipal core near 1,43,229 (queue figure 3,54,325, pending reconciliation); East Khasi Hills 3,25,388.

What is the pincode of Shillong?

The city starts at 793001; Shillong spans 793001–793109 across Laitumkhrah, Police Bazar and Mawlai.

Which places should I visit there?

Ward’s Lake, Shillong Peak, Elephant Falls and Bara Bazar — with Cherrapunji’s living-root bridges and Dawki as the weekend runs.
