Gandhinagar in one minute

Gandhinagar — live weather, population, hidden places and cost of living, auto-updated.

Gandhinagar is one of India’s cleanest planned capitals — built in the 1960s across a numbered sector grid on the Sabarmati’s left bank, named for Gandhi and shaded by avenues. The Akshardham temple’s pink-sandstone complex (1992) anchors its faith tourism, and the Mahatma Mandir convention floors host the state’s investor summits. Census 2011 counted about 2.1 lakh in the corporation, with the UA-adjacent belt grown past it.

The capital works in offices and ideas — Infocity’s IT campuses, the secretariat’s swarnim parkways and the rising GIFT City finance towers between here and Ahmedabad — while the Indroda park’s dinosaur-egg fossil belt gives the grid a prehistoric twist.

FAQ

What is Gandhinagar famous for?

The planned sector-grid capital, the Akshardham temple complex, Infocity’s IT park, the Mahatma Mandir summits and the rising GIFT City towers.

What is the population of Gandhinagar?

Census 2011: about 2,08,299 in the corporation city (queue figure 2,92,167 reflects later growth, pending reconciliation); UA ~4,16,000; district 13,87,478 pre-split.

What is the pincode of Gandhinagar?

The city starts at 382010; Gandhinagar spans 382010–382428 across the sector grid, Infocity and GIFT.

Which places should I visit there?

Akshardham’s complex, the Mahatma Mandir museum, Indroda’s fossil park and the river gardens — with Ahmedabad and Adalaj as the day runs.
